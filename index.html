05 / TIMELESS, NOT DISTANT

Old wisdom.
Your everyday world.

From the choices on your screen
to the direction of your future.

✎ 01

Education

Choose courses and learning paths carefully. Match them to your interests, goals and available time.

Ask yourself +

Would this course build a skill I actually want to use?

↗ 02

Career

Analyze opportunities before accepting them. Consider learning, experience and future value.

Ask yourself +

Which opportunity fits both my goals and my current needs?

₹ 03

Money

Think before spending or investing. Consider need, budget, alternatives and risk.

Ask yourself +

If this were not on sale, would I still need it?

♡ 04

Relationships

Understand situations before reacting emotionally. Listen first, then choose your words.

Ask yourself +

What might I be missing from the other person’s perspective?

⌘ 05

Technology

Consider consequences before posting or sharing information. Check its truth and respect privacy.

Ask yourself +

Would I share this if my name were attached to its consequences?

⚑ 06

Leadership

Evaluate risks before making decisions affecting others. Hear different views, then lead with clarity.

Ask yourself +

Who will be affected, and whose input have I not heard?
